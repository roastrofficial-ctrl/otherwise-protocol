## Extraction boundary

The candidate rolling lease and Sodium sealed-box choice are **new design proposals**, not claims that those exact constructions were tested in the research. Research motivates scope binding, cautious time handling, custody honesty, retained acceptance and the cancellation boundary. Future fixtures and independent implementation must establish the new profile's behavior.

Stable concepts survive; historical mistakes remain evidence. Multi-contract policy, retained witness committees, asynchronous Byzantine collection, two custody domains, DLEQ, consensus and membership recovery remain research capabilities rather than mandatory Solo dependencies. The proposal imports no source code or fixture private keys.

## Targeted approval amendments

The 29 October 2026 review strengthens three existing lines of provenance: 0.2/0.6 persistence-boundary and lost-acknowledgement work motivates exact terminal archive recovery; 0.4 finite scheduling and 0.8 survival limitations motivate explicit bounded-continuity warnings; 0.5 backup/device-loss and 0.8 offline recipient work motivate synthetic creator/recipient backup restoration without new account authority. New Phase B experiments must substantiate these obligations; old results do not establish them for Solo.
